# 无穷Grassmann流形的上同调群

**Theorem 7.1.** 
$$
H^*(G_n(\mathbb{R}^\infty);\mathbb{Z}_2) = \mathbb{Z}_2\big[w_1(\gamma^n),\dots,w_n(\gamma^n)\big]
$$
**Theorem 12.4.** 
$$
H^*(\tilde{G_n}(\mathbb{R}^\infty);\mathbb{Z}_2) = \mathbb{Z}_2\big[w_2(\tilde{\gamma}^n),\dots,w_n(\tilde{\gamma}^n)\big]
$$
**Theorem 14.5.** 
$$
H^*(G_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty);\mathbb{Z}_2) = \mathbb{Z}\big[c_1(\gamma^n),\dots,c_n(\gamma^n)\big]
$$
**Theorem 15.9.** 
$$
H^*\left(\tilde{G}_{2m+1}(\mathbb{R}^\infty);\mathbb{Z}\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]\right)=\mathbb{Z}\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]\big[p_1(\tilde{\gamma}^{2m+1}),\dots,p_m(\tilde{\gamma}^{2m+1})\big] \\
H^*\left(\tilde{G}_{2m}(\mathbb{R}^\infty);\mathbb{Z}\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]\right)=\mathbb{Z}\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]\big[p_1(\tilde{\gamma}^{2m+1}),\dots,p_{m-1}(\tilde{\gamma}^{2m+1}),e(\tilde{\gamma}^{2m+1})\big]
$$
